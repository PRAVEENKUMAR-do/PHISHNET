Security FAQ

Common questions about PhishNet

❓ What is PhishNet and what does it do?

▼

PhishNet is an ML-based malicious URL detection system that classifies any website link as Legitimate or Phishing/Malicious using advanced feature extraction and trained models providing high-accuracy detection and optimized performance.

❓ What features does PhishNet extract?

▼

It extracts 20 structural features including length, entropy, HTTPS usage, IP presence, subdomain count, and suspicious keywords. No live network lookups are needed.

❓ Why is there a "Proceed with Caution" option?

▼

No ML model is 100% accurate. The proceed option lets experienced users visit the URL after acknowledging the risk of a false positive.

❓ What dataset was used to train PhishNet?

▼

PhishNet was trained on diverse phishing datasets containing thousands of labelled URLs predicting benign, phishing, malware, and defacements to ensure advanced threat intelligence.
